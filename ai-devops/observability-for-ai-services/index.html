Observability for AI services

Combine conventional service health with model quality, usage, and data freshness.

Visual explanation

Deep dive

Keep service and model signals distinct

Availability, latency, and error rate describe the service. Groundedness, task success, refusal, and drift describe behavior or input quality. One can degrade while the other looks healthy.

Track cost drivers

Measure requests, tokens or equivalent usage, retries, model tier, and cost by approved aggregate. Set budgets and alert before a usage spike becomes an unplanned expense.

Protect telemetry

Use correlation IDs and redacted metadata where possible. Avoid collecting raw prompts or outputs by default; define access, retention, and audit policy for any sensitive trace.

Try it: Draw a dashboard with one service metric, one quality metric, one cost metric, and one freshness indicator. Explain what action a threshold should trigger.
Safety and reliability: Never expose prompts, outputs, customer data, or credentials in broadly accessible telemetry.
Mastery checkpoint: Explain "Observability for AI services", name one failure mode, and identify the relevant safety boundary.
