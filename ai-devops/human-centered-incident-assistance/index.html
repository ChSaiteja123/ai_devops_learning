Human-centered incident assistance

Use an assistant to organize approved evidence while responders retain diagnosis and change authority.

Visual explanation

Deep dive

Separate facts and hypotheses

A useful incident summary labels timestamps, observed signals, source references, and unknowns. Keep causal explanations clearly marked as hypotheses until evidence supports them.

Use read-only scope first

Read-only access can still expose sensitive information and be manipulated by log content. Filter scope and minimize data before sending it to a model.

Preserve incident command

The on-call responder owns triage and escalation. Any remediation uses established approval, testing, and rollback paths; an AI assistant should never silently restart or reconfigure systems.

Try it: Write a five-line fictional incident summary with two observations, one hypothesis, one unknown, and a human next step.
Safety and reliability: Do not grant an incident assistant unrestricted shell, secrets, or autonomous production remediation.
Mastery checkpoint: Explain "Human-centered incident assistance", name one failure mode, and identify the relevant safety boundary.
