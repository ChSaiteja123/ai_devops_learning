Where AI fits in the DevOps lifecycle

Map AI assistance to delivery work without confusing suggestions with approvals.

Visual explanation

Deep dive

Use a lifecycle view

Plan, code, build, test, release, operate, and learn are connected activities. AI may help draft or summarize at several points, but it does not replace accountable owners.

Match automation to risk

A suggestion in a disposable practice project has different risk from a production change. Increase review and approval as impact, scope, and irreversibility grow.

Measure outcomes

Evaluate whether AI improves a defined outcome such as review time or incident comprehension. Include error rate, rework, privacy, cost, and user impact, not speed alone.

Try it: Choose one low-risk development task where an assistant could help. Name the human reviewer and the test that would verify the suggestion.
Safety and reliability: Treat generated code, commands, plans, and incident explanations as untrusted until independently reviewed.
