Release strategies and rollback

Promote changes gradually and define observable stop and rollback conditions.

Visual explanation

Deep dive

Promote immutable artifacts

Build once, identify the exact artifact and configuration, and promote that same version through environments. Avoid rebuilding a different artifact after approval.

Canary with a comparison

Expose a small, defined cohort to a change and compare it with a suitable baseline. Watch service errors, latency, resource use, and relevant quality measures.

Rollback is designed in advance

Define who can stop promotion, which signals trigger rollback, how to restore a known-good version, and how to verify recovery. A rollback is not assumed to undo data side effects.

Try it: For a fictional model-backed help page, name two canary signals, one stop threshold, and the role authorized to halt promotion.
