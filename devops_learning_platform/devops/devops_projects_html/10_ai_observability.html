10. AI Observability

Monitor latency, errors, tokens, retrieval, tools, and safety signals.

Purpose

AI systems need platform telemetry plus model-quality and cost signals. A healthy container can still produce poor or expensive answers.

Signals

request rate | time to first token | total latency | errors | token usage retrieval hit rate | tool failures | refusal rate | safety events

Run

docker compose up -d # Prometheus: http://localhost:9090
[IMPORTANT] Every actionable alert needs an owner, threshold, runbook, and escalation path.
