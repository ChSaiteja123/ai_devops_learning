User: How do I troubleshoot a failing Kubernetes deployment?
Assistant: Check pod events, describe the deployment, inspect logs, and verify readiness probes and resource limits before changing the manifest.
User: What should I check first?
Assistant: Start with kubectl get pods, kubectl describe pod, and kubectl logs to isolate whether the issue is scheduling, image pull, or app startup.
